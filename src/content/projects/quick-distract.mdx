<Section label="summary">
While launching their augmented reality storybooks, the team at [SpellBound AR](https://www.spellboundar.com/) discovered an unexpected market: pediatric hospitals. Child life specialists found the books to be powerful tools for distracting young patients during anxiety-inducing procedures.

That insight led to launching Quick Distract Cards — SpellBound AR's first product line built specifically for hospital use. Each card gives child life specialists a fast, no-setup tool that helps them transform a hospital room into a place of wonder and excitement with just a smartphone.
</Section>

<Section label="my role">
As the project's game designer and developer, I owned the experience from concept to App Store: designing AR interactions for each card type, defining the visual style for our art assets, and building everything in Unity using the [Vuforia Engine](https://developer.vuforia.com/home).

When child life specialists surfaced new needs in the field, I translated those needs into product pitches and delivered the designs, which spanned form factors from collectible trading cards to immersive playsets and room-scale wall decals.
</Section>
